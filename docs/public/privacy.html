Privacy Policy / 隐私政策

Effective date: to be set before release.

Data Center Tycoon stores game progress locally on your device. The base game does not require an account or a game server.

If rewarded advertising is enabled in the distributed build, the advertising and consent providers may process device, advertising, interaction, and diagnostic information according to their disclosed policies and your consent choices. The final provider names and links will be inserted after P04 is confirmed.

Apple processes in-app purchases. The game receives purchase status and product identifiers but does not receive your payment-card details.

Starting a new company from Settings resets run progress while retaining purchases, premium currency, achievements, and permanent prestige bonuses in the local account state. Deleting the app removes local data, subject to device backups managed by Apple; non-consumable purchases can be restored through the Store.

《机房大亨》的游戏进度仅保存在设备本地，基础游戏不要求账号或联网后端。若发行包启用激励广告，广告与同意管理服务可能依据其政策及你的同意选项处理设备、广告、互动和诊断信息。内购由 Apple 处理，游戏不会取得银行卡信息。“新公司”会重置本局进度，但保留本地账户中的购买记录、付费货币、成就与永久转生加成；删除 App 会移除本地数据，非消耗型购买可在商店恢复。
